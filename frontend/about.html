About StudyMate AI

StudyMate AI is engineered for students who demand high efficiency. By combining cognitive science principles—such as active recall, Socratic questioning, and spaced repetition—with state-of-the-art language models, StudyMate transforms raw lecture slides and textbooks into interactive learning experiences.

Whether you are preparing for medical board exams, computer science algorithms, or university finals, StudyMate AI empowers you to study smarter and achieve higher retention in less time.
