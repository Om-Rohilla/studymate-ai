Quantum Mechanics — Chapter 4 Summary

Format: Structured Outline

1. Wave-Particle Duality

Light and matter display behavior resembling both waves and particles depending on measurement method.

  • de Broglie Wavelength: λ = h / p
  • Photoelectric Effect: Demonstrates particle nature of photons (E = hf).

2. Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle

It is impossible to simultaneously measure both position (x) and momentum (p) of a particle with absolute precision.

  • Formula: Δx · Δp ≥ ℏ / 2
